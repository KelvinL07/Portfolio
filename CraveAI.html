Project Overview

The Crave project encompasses several key components aimed at delivering a comprehensive AI-driven food recognition and recommendation system.

AI & Recognition

The core is an AI Image Recognition model capable of accurately identifying and categorizing food images. Trained on a diverse deep learning dataset, it classifies common meals and exotic dishes. We leverage Google Drive for scalable cloud storage of training assets.

Web Application

Built with Streamlit, the front-end offers a seamless user experience. Users upload food images to receive instant predictions. The system also features a taste-based recommendation engine, analyzing user preferences to suggest personalized meals.

Global Reach

We emphasize open-source contribution and internationalization, ensuring the platform is accessible to non-English speakers and adaptable to diverse cultural needs.
